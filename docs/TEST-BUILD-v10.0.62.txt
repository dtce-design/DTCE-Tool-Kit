DTCE Tool Kit v10.0.62 (28 November 2026) - test build; not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (needs a new explicit request).

PATCH - Design Loads tabs, automatic rho, report scrolling after switching apps:
- Design Loads (owner: "yung steps sa design load alisin mo, ilipat mo na lang doon yung primary loads ng load combo, pati settings"): the
  "Steps (how to use Design Loads)" sub tab is removed. The first sub tabs are now "Primary loads (Load Combinations)" and "Settings (Load
  Combinations)" - the Primary loads and Settings pages of the Load Combinations sheet, shown at the Design Loads level. They remain part of
  the Load Combinations sheet (same inputs, saving, links and report); the Load Combinations tab keeps its other pages.
- Redundancy factor rho (owner: "automatic ba ito?"): rho is computed by itself 0.7 s after the STAAD model on the Primary loads tab, the
  member end forces or the other rho inputs change, and whenever the rho tab is shown; "Recompute rho" stays for a manual run.
- Report pages (owner: "yung scroll ng report kapag lumilipat ako ng ibang window na ibang app bukod sa tool kit"): the scroll range is also
  rebuilt when the Tool Kit window is activated again from another app (the window itself, not the design sheet inside it) and when the
  report gets the focus.
Tests: StaadCombinationsTests 38, RedundancyFactorTests 20, WindAuditTests 79, RsaCurveTests 23.
Includes everything of v10.0.61.
