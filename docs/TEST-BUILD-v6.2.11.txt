PATCH (Column Formatter; owner: "pwede ka ba gumawa din ng reference elevation per column.. gaya doon sa rcdc", COLUMNS_WALLS.dxf
"based din dito"):
- Column elevations (option, on by default): a DETAIL OF COLUMN per column mark at 1:50 below the schedule, as RCDC's: the floors with
  their elevations (TOC), the column outline per level, the ties at their spacing (first tie 50 from each face, the Z1 spacing over
  Lo at each end, the rest between) with the zone dimensions and the tie labels, the main bars with the bar labels, the laps, 90°
  hooks at the top and dowel hooks at the bottom, and SECTION 1-1 beside it with the bar legend. In both the standard and ACI layouts.
- Lap position option as RCDC's lapping position: Slab top (just above each floor, default) or Mid height.
- Beam depths at the joints are not in the column schedule, so the joints are not drawn. The Bar Bending Schedule still takes the
  laps just above each floor.
Checked on Linux/Mono only with schedule.dxf and test_rcdcd.dxf; not opened in AutoCAD here.
