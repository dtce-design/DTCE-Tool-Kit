DTCE Tool Kit v1.28.0 test build (18 November 2026) - new behaviour

Construction Schedule diagrams: continuity, standard notation, no overlapping text
(owner: "continuity ng diagrams per sheets nakakalito", "yung diagram continuity ang
need ko hindi off page connector", "hindi to sheet ang need, yung connector lang ng
lines arrows", "nagkaroon na ng overlapping ng text", "yung diagrams ba namin as per
standard?")
- One continuous drawing per network, cut into A3 sheets at match lines (thin
  dash-dot line). The "to n (sheet s)" / "from n (sheet s)" tags and the repeated
  events are removed. Every line that reaches a match line continues on the next
  sheet at the same height; the joins are placed in widened gaps between columns, so
  only straight horizontal lines cross a join - no bend, node, event or text is ever
  on or split by a join. To join the printed sheets, trim along a match line and butt
  the next sheet to it.
- PDM (activity on node; DPWH uses PDM): relation labels show type and lag only
  ("SS +3", "FF +1", "FS +7"); FS without lag is not labelled (usual convention);
  relations of the same type and lag share one arrowhead. Where several items of one
  trade lead to several items of a later trade, the relations pass through one trade
  milestone (M1, M2 ... "Reinforcing steel complete", D = 0) - same CPM times, far
  fewer lines. Every finish that has no successor (only SS work after it) is joined
  to FINISH, so no finish is left open. Lines are routed in free lanes and step
  between them in the gaps before columns: no line runs behind a node or a label.
- PERT-CPM (activity on arrow): orthogonal arrows out of the right side and into the
  left side of the events; dummies (no time) dashed, lag arrows (time, no work)
  dash-dot and labelled "SS +3"; event numbers i < j, no two activities share i-j; a
  lag arrow is added wherever an FF relation sets the start of an activity, so a
  forward pass on the drawn arrows gives exactly the TE printed in the circles. The
  legend states that with a = 0.8 m and b = 1.2 m, te = m (PERT duration = CPM
  duration) and that sigma / P90 / P95 rest on the assumed +/-20 % spread. Levels with
  many events are split into staggered sub-columns.
- Automatic drawing checks (tests): no label overlaps a label or a node, no line
  through a node, nothing split or bent at a join, no label cut short - for this
  project and for networks of every 2nd / 3rd / 4th catalogue item. Remaining in the
  densest networks only: a few dashed lines pass behind a label (the label keeps its
  white background and stays legible). With all 268 catalogue items at once (246
  activities), the PERT sheet still has some crossings; the PDM has none.

Includes v1.27.2. Not tested on Windows. Not a Microsoft Store release.
