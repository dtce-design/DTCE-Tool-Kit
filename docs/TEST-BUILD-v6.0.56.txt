PATCH (Bar Bending Schedule; owner: "if hindi isinama sa estimate dapat yung cut ng main is hanggang mag lap"):
- A span left out of the estimate splits the main (continuous) bars: each included part of the line is laid out on its own and
  ends one Class B lap inside the left-out span as dowels (abang). The main bars run in one length and are spliced only where
  their length needs it (before, the laps planned for the whole line stayed, e.g. a needless lap and its extra hoops in the
  next span). Pieces that end as dowels say "dowel (abang) into span n".
- Side face bars of a left-out span are no longer listed.
Checked on Linux/Mono only.

Includes v6.0.55 and earlier builds.
