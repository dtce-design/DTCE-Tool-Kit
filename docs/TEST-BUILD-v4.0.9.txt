PATCH: extends the existing Bar Bending Schedule workspace (beams).

Beam elevation and sections in the printed BBS, on A3 ("Hindi mo sinama yung section ng beam at elevation sa report? Para ma
visualize clearly? Gamit ng A3 para ok")
- The printed bar bending schedule is now A3 landscape (print, preview and PDF). Other reports stay A4.
- New section 3 "Beam details - elevation and sections", for every beam line:
  - Elevation: supports S1, S2 ..., beam outline and slab, every bar on its own lane with a leader to its mark, number, size
    and cut length, 90° hooks at the exterior supports, laps (orange, NG in red), allowed splice zones (green), hoop ticks and
    hoop zones (legs, size, spacing), section cuts, support widths, clear spans with the beam mark and size, the 2h hoop
    zones at every face, and the overall length. Depth is drawn larger than the horizontal scale for clarity.
  - Sections 1A / 1B / 1C ... at the left face, midspan and right face of every span at a standard scale (1:10, 1:15 ...):
    concrete, slab line, hoop with 135° hooks and crossties, top and bottom bars at their diameter with their count and size,
    side-face bars, width / depth dimensions, hoop spacing at that point, cover, and the bar marks present.
- Sections renumbered: 4 Bar bending schedule, 5 Weight summary, 6 Cutting list, 7 SMF check, 8 Splice zone check.

Includes v4.0.8 (was v1.49.8) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
