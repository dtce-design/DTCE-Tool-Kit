PATCH: Wall Finishes without Wall ID, Side and Area basis.

- Wall Finishes ("sa wall finish hayaan mo na pala yung wall ID, huwag mo na isama. pati yung side and area basis"; "kasi pwedeng
  madaming wall ID ang iassign ko sa isang finish sa isang room"): the Wall ID, Side and Area basis columns are hidden and the
  "Show walls on DXF" button is not added. A wall finish is measured by its own Length x Height x Qty (one face); one row can cover
  several walls of a room.
- Saved projects open unchanged: the hidden values stay in the same columns and older rows keep computing with them (for example a
  row on "Whole wall (auto)" or Side "Both"). Blank values are read as Side A and Dimensions.

Includes v5.1.2 and earlier builds (see their notes).
