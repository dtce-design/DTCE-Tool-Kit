PATCH (Column Formatter; owner: "yung drawing ng columns gayahin kagaya ng mismong dxf", "no, use rcdc version", "option if closed
ties or open ties ang gagamitin doon sa extra", "create a legend per bar size"):
- The sections are drawn as the RCDC schedule draws them: its tie polylines (rounded corners, 135° hook tails), its bar rings and the
  mark of each bar size (e.g. a line for Ø20, a filled dot for Ø16), taken from the source DXF and drawn to scale in each cell.
- Bar legend: one mark per bar size for the whole schedule, under REMARKS (the RCDC mark of the size; a symbol for sizes RCDC did
  not draw).
- Extra ties option: Closed ties (as RCDC) or Open ties (crossties) - each inner closed tie becomes two crossties with a 135° and a
  90° hook, in the drawing, in the ties per set and in the Bar Bending Schedule; the choice is written in the NOTES and read back.
- The ties and bars stay exact in the file (bar size in the ring layer, each tie as two points), so the revised DXF reads back
  unchanged in the Bar Bending Schedule and the Bill of Quantities.
Checked on Linux/Mono only with the owner's schedule.dxf.

Includes v6.2.3 and earlier builds.
