Truss Support on RC - interactive model ("Gawin mong interactive ang input may model")
- The plan and elevation model is now at the top of the Plate and anchors tab and edits the inputs it shows:
  drag the plate edges (B, N), the RC support edges, an anchor (up / down = gauge, left / right = pitch), the gusset
  ends (length) and top (height) and the anchor tip (hef); values move in 5 mm steps and every check and the report
  update at once. Double-click a blue dimension to type its value (Enter to apply, Esc to cancel). Handles are
  highlighted under the mouse.
- Parts whose checks are NG are drawn red (plate, anchors, gusset), and the failing groups are named under the
  elevation.

Includes v4.0.1 (was v1.49.1) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
