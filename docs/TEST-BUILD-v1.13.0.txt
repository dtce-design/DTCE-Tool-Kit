DTCE Tool Kit v1.13.0 test build (18 November 2026)

Sticky pay item in Bill of Quantities (owner: "kapag naka-dropdown, maglolock yung
position ng cell, gagalaw yung nasa loob, at mag-unlock pag naabot ang last")
- While you scroll through the rows of an expanded pay item, that pay item
  (item no., description, quantity, unit) stays pinned just under the column
  headers, with a blue line under it.
- It is released as soon as its last sub row has scrolled past and the next
  pay item comes up. Click the pinned row to jump back to the pay item.

Includes v1.12.0. Not a Microsoft Store release (v1.5.0 is the Store build).
