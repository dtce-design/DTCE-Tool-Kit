DTCE Tool Kit v7.1.25 (23 November 2026) - test build; not uploaded to the Microsoft Store.

PATCH - Staircase Design drawings and report (owner: "wala na talagang straight bar yung top bars?"; "ayusin din sa reports, mas malaki
para makita lahat ng details"; "complete detailed calculation report"; open-well 3D "ayusin"; slabless section "fix"; plan "reflect din
yung dimensions"):
- New input Top bars: Full length (default - straight along the whole flight into the supports, crossing at the lower landing) or At
  the supports (L/4). The reinforcement table and the B callout follow the choice.
- Report figures are drawn at screen size and scaled into the page (no overlapping labels); the sections are larger (380 pt).
- New report section 4A DETAILED CALCULATION for every member: factored loads per segment, overhang, reactions, Mu+ / Mu- / Vu, d,
  Rn, rho, As required / minimum, bar spacing, a and phiMn, top bars, one-way shear phiVc, minimum thickness, distribution bars, hook
  and development lengths - each with the formula, the values and the result (OK / NG).
- 3D: each step of a waist-slab flight is its own block with the straight sloped soffit, so open-well and dog-legged flights no longer
  draw over each other.
- Slabless / folded plate section: both bar layers follow the treads and risers (zigzag) with distribution bars at every fold, and
  the plate underside follows the profile.
- Plan: dimension chains of the flights, landings and gap along the bottom and the left side, the overall length and width, and
  "n treads x tread = going" on each flight.
