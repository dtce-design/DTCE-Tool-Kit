PATCH: no lockset / hinges for aluminum doors and windows.

- Door & Window Schedule ("no locket hinges if aluminum"): rows with Material = Aluminum get 0 locksets / leversets and
  0 hinges (the aluminum frame comes with its own hardware), even if a per-unit count was typed. They are left out of the
  Finishing Hardware (1004(2)) quantities. Wood, PVC and Steel doors and windows keep their automatic locksets and hinges.
