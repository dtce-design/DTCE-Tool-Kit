PATCH: Strap Foundation - sliding and overturning ("sa strap design include check in sliding overturning").

- Overturning (service cases): each group of footings tied by straps (or a footing on its own) is checked tipping about the
  outer edge of its footings toward +X, -X, +Z and -Z (+Y / -Y when Z is up). Overturning moment = column moments + horizontal
  reactions x the STAAD support height above the base; resisting moment = column loads, footing + soil and strap weights x
  their distance to that edge. FS = Mr / Mo >= the required FS (new input, default 1.5). The governing direction and case are
  reported with the derivation.
- Sliding: per footing (μ R / H) and, for strapped footings, for the whole group (μ ΣR / ΣH, the straps tie the footings);
  required FS is a new input (default 1.5). The per-footing sliding check now also runs when only service cases are given.
- A group without horizontal reactions or column moments is noted instead of checked.

Includes v5.0.5 (was v1.50.5) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
