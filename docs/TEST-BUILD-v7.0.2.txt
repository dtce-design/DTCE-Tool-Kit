PATCH - Response Spectrum (RSA) design sheet, Earthquake tab (owner: "based doon sa research ni Wells and Coppersmith pano yun
mineasure, apply sa mga fault line ng PH. isama ito as basis sa pagpili ng Seismic Source Type"; "hindi ba niya pwede sukatin yung
nasa inupload kong kmz file?").
- Wells and Coppersmith (1994) basis: magnitude on surface rupture length, all slip types, M = 5.08 + 1.16 log10(L), 77 earthquakes,
  standard deviation 0.28, L 1.3 to 432 km (shallow crustal earthquakes; most relations do not differ significantly with the style
  of faulting). L is the surface rupture length of one earthquake, so on a Philippine fault it is the length of the named segment /
  continuous strand that would rupture at once, measured along its trace; parallel strands are not added together.
- New input "Fault rupture length L km": filled from the fault trace; typed values stay and give Mmax.
- A HazardHunterPH fault that is not in the built-in traces (e.g. Dummon River Fault System) no longer takes another fault's length:
  the sheet says so; load the PHIVOLCS fault map (KMZ) and click the fault, or enter L.
- PHIVOLCS fault map KMZ: the red active fault lines (solid, dashed, dotted offshore projections) are traced from the map images and
  measured; legend samples and the logo are left out; black "potentially active" faults are not traced (black is also label text).
  Click a fault line on the earthquake map to take it as the site's fault; Ctrl+click adds a part (a fault interrupted on the map).
  The site's fault is drawn in blue.
- Seismic source type: Table 208-3 is also checked at Mmax - 0.28 and Mmax + 0.28; when the type changes within that range the
  summary and the report say so ("confirm Mmax with PHIVOLCS"). Subduction interfaces and L outside 1.3 to 432 km are flagged.
- Report section 00 shows the relation, L, where L came from, and the +/- 0.28 check. The fault estimate is saved with the sheet.
