  has the STAAD input, the member loads on the recessed sides and its LOAD numbers (otherwise numbered 901-904 to adjust).
- Design sheets keep the order their inputs were added in ("bat yung guide ng no. 3 nasa baba ng 4"): a group's note can no longer
  slip under the next heading (seen on Windows in the Wind building inputs).
