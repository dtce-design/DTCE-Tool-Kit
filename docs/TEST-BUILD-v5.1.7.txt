PATCH: bars per layer for every width; effective depth with layers checked.

- Bars per layer ("sure ka for every width ng beam may certain no. lang ang kasya per layer"): yes - n = floor((inside + c) / (db + c)),
  inside = b - 2 cover - 2 stirrup Ø, c = max(25 mm, db, 4/3 aggregate). With 40 mm cover, Ø10 stirrups and 20 mm aggregate:
    b 250: Ø16 4, Ø20 3, Ø25 3 | b 300: Ø16 5, Ø20 4, Ø25 4 | b 350: Ø16 6, Ø20 5, Ø25 5 | b 400: Ø16 7, Ø20 7, Ø25 6
    b 450: Ø16 8, Ø20 8, Ø25 7 | b 500: Ø16 10, Ø20 9, Ø25 8.
  Fix: a section narrower than two bars plus one clear space used to report 2 bars per layer; it now reports 1 (used by the Strap
  Foundation checks and the Bar Bending Schedule layering).
- Effective depth with layers ("icheck din ba if pumasa yung no. of layers yung effective depth"): already in the strap flexure check -
  d = h - cover - stirrup Ø - centroid of the bar layers (25 mm clear between layers), shown in the steps; more layers give a smaller d
  and a larger required steel. Example: 800 mm strap, 8-25 in 6 + 2 layers -> d = 725 mm (one layer of 4-25: d = 737.5 mm). Tests added.
