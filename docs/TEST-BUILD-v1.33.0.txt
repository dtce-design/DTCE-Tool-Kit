Construction Schedule

  the work starts ("habang inaapply pwede na magstart ang project").

  demobilization after the last work (5 days by default) ("include also mobilization and
  demobilization"). Double-click these rows to change their durations.
- Bar Chart headings stay at the top while the rows scroll.
