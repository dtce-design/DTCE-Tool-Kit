DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.34 test build (21 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (lintels over openings and rebar offcuts):
- Own Bill of Quantities item: the automatic lintels are "Lintel Beam over Openings concrete" and "Lintel Beam over Openings forms"
  (concrete pay item 900(1)i, forms 903(2)), apart from lintels entered by hand in the Lintel Beam sheet.
- The automatic lintels are shown at the top of Miscellaneous Structures > Lintel Beam (from the masonry walls and their openings,
  with concrete, formworks and RSB by diameter); the grid under them is only for other lintels entered by hand. The separate
  "Lintels over Openings" tab is removed.
- "Edit lintel reinforcement schedule": marks, CHB, largest clear opening, b x h, bottom / top / mid bars, tie type and spacing,
  bearing and lateral brace can be edited, checked and saved with the project; "Reset to the report" gives the Lintel Beam Design
  Calculation Report schedule back.
- Rebar offcuts: checked in every rebar take-off. The Bill of Quantities already uses one shared plan for beam, slab, footing, wall
  footing, RC wall, canopy, column / miscellaneous members (lintels included) and slope protection: short pieces are cut from the
  leftovers of other members of the same grade and diameter. The saving is now shown in every "Rebar by grade and diameter" tab and
  in the report (purchase shared vs. each workspace / member cut alone). Plumbing structures (canal, septic vault, catch basin)
  share offcuts among themselves only, as their own items.
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Tests: LintelScheduleTests (27), OpeningLintelTests (11), CommercialBarsTests (836).

Includes v6.0.33 and earlier builds.
