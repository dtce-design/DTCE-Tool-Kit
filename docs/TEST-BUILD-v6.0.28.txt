PATCH (Bar Bending Schedule; "sure ka sa splice zone ng bot at top? bat sa top walang cranked bars"; Curtailment Settings picture):
- Short spans in a special moment frame: the allowed splice zone (outside 2h from each face, NSCP 418.6.3.3) can be shorter than
  the Class B lap (e.g. Ln 4.000 m, h 800: zone 800 mm, Ø16 top lap 1550 mm). The top continuous bars used to stop after the first
  12 m piece ("NO VALID SPLICE"). Now the lap goes to midspan of the farthest span within reach, cranked, so the line keeps all its
  bars; the splice check marks it NG with "use Type 2 mechanical splices or a longer bar".
- Stirrup end zones follow the beam curtailment Shear Zones where the schedule gives no spacing text: ductile beam (special moment
  frame) D x 2, non-ductile L / 3 (new "Stirrup end zones" choice; "% of Ln" stays available). Cutting lists saved before keep
  their % of Ln.
- Confirmed against the Curtailment Settings: top L/4 at end and continuous supports; bottom midspan L/6, end and continuous
  supports L/4 (as before).
