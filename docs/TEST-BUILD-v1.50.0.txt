Strap Foundation ("Strap Foundation. Ready input ng reaction kay staad. Yung foundation niya adjustable lahat yung pagka
eccentric. Anong direction ng strap or double or triple any. Directions is same kay staad")
- STAAD reactions: paste or import the Support Reactions table (node, L/C, Fx, Fy, Fz, Mx, My, Mz). Axes are the STAAD global
  axes: Y up by default (plan X-Z, STAAD top view with +Z down the sheet); Z up is a choice (plan X-Y). Load cases named with
  load factors (1.2, 1.4, 1.6, 0.9) are factored, the others service; both lists can be typed.
- Footings: one per support node at its STAAD coordinates ("Paste STAAD node coordinates" fills X and Z from the Nodes table;
  "Add footings from the reaction nodes"). Each footing has its own column size, B x L x thickness, eccentricity ex and ez
  (footing centre minus column centre, either sign, both directions), "moment to" (Auto / Straps / Footing) and bars.
- Straps: any two columns, any direction (along X, along Z or diagonal); a footing can have one, two, three or more straps.
- Statics (rigid footings and straps, straps clear of the soil): at a footing whose moment goes to the straps the strap end
  moments make the soil pressure as uniform as the straps allow (two straps in different directions: uniform; one strap: the
  part across the strap stays with the footing); the other footings are the fulcrums. Classic two-footing case R = P L / (L - e).
  Column moments, horizontal reactions (times the support height above the base), footing, soil and strap weights included.
- Checks (NSCP 2015 / ACI 318-14): bearing and full contact (service, gross); footing flexure both directions with the off-centre
  column cantilevers and minimum steel; one-way shear; punching with interior / edge / corner perimeter (αs 40 / 30 / 20);
  strap top and bottom bars (As min), strap shear with stirrups and spacing.
- Live plan model of the layout; results tab; presentable report: basis, materials, footing and strap tables, plan in the STAAD
  axes, reactions, statics per load case, elevation of each strap with the factored moment and shear envelopes, every check as
  formula -> substitution -> result, summary, blank signatures and notes. Examples: exterior + interior (1 strap) and corner
  footing (2 straps).

Includes v1.49.13 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
