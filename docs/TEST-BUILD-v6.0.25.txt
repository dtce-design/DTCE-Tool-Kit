PATCH (beam elevation; "alisin mo na yung splice zone, label na lang or dimension ... ayusin mo na lang yung naka cranked na
lapping"):
- The allowed splice zones are no longer drawn in the beam: they are dimensions under it - "splice zone, top" and "splice zone,
  bottom" with their length - in the BBS report elevation and in the model.
- Lapped bars: the pieces of one bar stay on one lane, so a lap reads as the straight end of one piece beside the cranked start
  of the next, with the crank back to the bar line (before, the second piece went to another lane).
