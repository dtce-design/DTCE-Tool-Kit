PATCH: Design Sheets and Strap Foundation.

- "Base Plate & Anchor Bolts" removed from the Design Sheets ("yung base plate at anchor bolt sa truss support ata yun"; owner
  chose to remove it): base plates and anchor bolts are designed in Truss Support on RC.
- Strap Foundation:
  - Reactions per support ("yung input ng reactions ay per support"; owner chose a table per support): selecting a footing (in
    its table or in the plan) shows "Reactions at support N" with its load cases (Fx, Fy, Fz, Mx, My, Mz) to type or edit; the
    whole STAAD table can still be pasted in the first tab and is split per support. One store: editing a support's table
    rewrites only that support's rows of the STAAD table.
  - Footings and straps tab: tables on the left (footings, straps, reactions at the selected support), the model on the right
    (plan / 3D on top, the section of the selected strap below) ("yung sa strap tables sa left model sa right").
  - Bar arrangement in layers ("yung arrangement din ng bars sana if ilan layers"): the strap bars of a face go in as many per
    layer as the clear spacing allows (max(25, db, 4/3 aggregate), NSCP 425.2.1), the rest in the next layer directly above
    (425.2.2) with Ø25 spacer bars between layers. The flexure d uses the centroid of the layers; the check "bar arrangement"
    lists the layers (e.g. 10-25 = 7 + 3). The strap section (app and report) draws the layers, the spacer and the stirrup.

Includes v5.0.8 (was v1.50.8) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
