Construction Schedule - LS items
- Finishing (and rough) hardware, item 1004, has no installation of its own ("kasama na siya sa wooden
  doors and windows"): its bar runs with the door and window rows and it is not an activity of the
  network.
- The other LS items take the working hours entered by the owner ("input ko na lang if ilang hours"):
  double-click the row and enter the hours; days = hours / 8 (per crew), rounded up. The logic places
  the item with its trade. Mobilization and demobilization keep their durations in days.

Includes v1.35.0, v1.34.0 and v1.33.0 (see their notes).

Bar Chart ("sa bar chart ifix mo din yung manpower sa baba")

  the top and the item columns at the left). The printed set is unchanged.
