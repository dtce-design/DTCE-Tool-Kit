MINOR raised: a new design sheet (owner's numbering rule of 19 November 2026).

New design sheet: Base Plate & Anchor Bolts ("Design ng baseplate tapos anchor bolt")
- STAAD reactions tab: paste or import the Support Reactions table of the column bases (Fx, Fy, Fz, Mx, My, Mz;
  kN / kip / N / metric ton headings converted), choose the vertical axis and the moment that bends the plate along N,
  and the nodes to design. Every row is checked; the governing row is shown for each check.
- Column and plate: I / W, HSS rectangle or pipe column; plate B x N x tp and Fy; RC pedestal size and depth; fc'.
- Anchor bolts: two rows (one near each plate end), M16-M36, anchors per row, gauge, hef, headed or J/L hooked, futa /
  fya, cracked concrete, grout pad, optional friction 0.55 Pu on grout.
- Checks (LRFD): AISC Design Guide 1 - fp,max = φc 0.85 fc' √(A2/A1), small / large moment (e vs ecrit, Y, anchor
  tension Tu = qmax Y - Pu), "(f + N/2)² ≥ 2Pu(e + f)/qmax", plate thickness at the bearing interface (m, n, λn') and at
  the tension interface; uplift. NSCP 2015 Section 417: anchor steel tension and shear, concrete breakout in tension
  (tension row, or all anchors for uplift) and shear (toward the pedestal edge), pullout, pryout, tension-shear
  interaction. Results tab and calculation report with each row's Pu, Vu, Mu, e, case, Y and Tu.
- Column-to-plate welds, anchor reinforcement and the pedestal itself are not designed by this sheet (noted in the
  report). Uses the Truss Connections program access.

Includes v1.48.1 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
