DTCE Tool Kit v1.44.0 test build (19 November 2026) - not pushed (owner: "huwag mo munang ipush")

Not tested on Windows yet (Linux/Mono checks only). Not a Microsoft Store release.

Cutting List workspace - Beam Cutting List ("gawa ka din ng beam cutting list ... another workspace ito";
"Upload lang ng schedule, set up na lang ng dimensions span supports etc. lagyan na lang ng check if ano yung
isasama sa estimate per span. Splice same na sa design sheet ng splices. Icheck yung splicing zones as per
nscp. Generate cutting list and estimates")
- New "Cutting List" card on the Workspaces window (uses the Program of Works access).
- Load the beam schedule DXF (formatted or manual schedules; several files allowed). Enter the beam lines one
  row per span: line ID, beam mark, clear span, left / right support widths, slab thickness, identical lines
  and an "In estimate" tick.
- Lap, development and hook lengths come from the chosen Splice, Development & Hooks configuration (the same
  values as that design sheet; top-bar factor for top bars).
- Continuous top and bottom bars run the whole line with 90-degree hooks into the exterior supports and are
  spliced only in the allowed zones: special moment frame per NSCP 2015 418.6.3.3 (no lap in a joint or within
  2h of a joint face, hoops over the lap at not more than d/4 or 100 mm - checked against the stirrup spacing);
  ordinary frame option (top bars in the middle third, bottom bars over interior supports). Pieces never exceed
  the chosen longest bar. Hooked ends are checked against Ldh and the support width; stirrup end zones against
  2h (418.6.4.1).
- Extra top bars over supports (Ln/3 or Ln/4 past the face), extra bottom bars, side-face bars, stirrups and
  crossties per span (both manual L/M/R and formatted "1-50mm, n-s mm, REST AT" spacings).
- Results: cutting list (bar mark, location, cut length, pieces, shape, weight), splice zone check, estimate per
  span (rebar by diameter, stirrup sets, concrete and forms below the slab; unticked spans are left out of the
  estimate only), commercial bars to buy, review list; print preview / PDF report; copy; save / open
  (.beam-cutting.xml).
- Column, foundation and slab cutting lists are next.

Includes v1.43.0 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
