PATCH: Strap Foundation - visualization ("Mas maayos na visualization").

- 3D view: turn it by dragging (yaw / pitch), zoom with the wheel, "Reset 3D view"; footing tops coloured by the soil pressure
  of the shown load case with a q / qa colour scale; "Show / hide reinforcement (3D)" shows the concrete see-through with the
  footing bottom mesh both ways (red along X, blue across), the strap top bars (red), bottom bars (green) and the stirrups.
- Plan: grid lines through the columns with bubbles (1, 2, 3 ... along X; A, B, C ... across) and dimension chains of the column
  spacing; each footing coloured by its soil pressure (cells from q avg and the pressure gradient) with the q / qa scale; red
  outline for a failing footing; labels moved clear of the straps.
- Strap elevation: top and bottom bars, stirrups at their spacing and the footing bottom bars drawn with their labels; moment
  values at both column faces; clearer spacing.
- Report: plan with the pressure map and grid, 3D view with pressures, 3D reinforcement view, strap elevations with bars.
