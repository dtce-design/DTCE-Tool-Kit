PATCH (Column Formatter; owner: "pangit, sundan mismo yung paglayout ng sample file ko lalo na sa open ties"):
- The schedule follows the owner's Drawing1.dxf: SCHEDULE OF COLUMNS with the NOTES (fy with its grade, concrete and cover) above,
  four marks per block (COLUMN MARKED, 68 wide level columns from the lowest, 79 high rows: the section with its dimensions above an
  18 high text cell), blocks side by side, merged cells for the same section at neighbouring levels, crossed-out empty levels, the
  continuation triangle between neighbouring sections and the bar legend under REMARKS at the top right and the bottom left.
- Column marks are C1, C2, C3 ... (prefix + number) as in the sample.
- Sections drawn as the sample: bar circles with the mark of their size (largest X, next a vertical line, next a filled dot), the
  hoop around the bars with its 135° hook at the top left (a half circle and two parallel tails), dimensions with circle ticks.
- Open ties as the sample: each inner link is two lapped C-shaped ties, each with a 135° hook at one end and a straight tail at the
  other. Closed ties: each inner link one closed tie with a 135° hook. The cell text keeps "(3 TIES/SET)" as the sample.
- The cell text is the sample's four lines (no Lo line). Lo, the Z1 spacing of the other links, the original marks and the tie option
  are kept as hidden data (layer DTCE-DATA, off and not plotted), so the revised DXF still reads back unchanged in the Bar Bending
  Schedule and the Bill of Quantities.
- Line weights as the sample: concrete 0.30, bar marks 0.15, ties, bars and dimensions 0.09.
