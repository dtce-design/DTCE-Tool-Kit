DTCE Tool Kit v10.0.37 (27 November 2026) - test build; not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (the Store upload is on hold).

PATCH - terrain aligned with the building by itself:
- Wind Load sheet ("yung totoo naka align na ba ito sa building? bakit naka N, NE?"; "pati sa kzt"): with building pressures on, the
  eight directions of the terrain are taken from the ridge azimuth (faces and diagonals, since v7.2.37) - but a terrain loaded before the
  ridge azimuth was set (or an older saved sheet) stayed at N, NE, E ... and only showed a message. Now the terrain is loaded again by
  itself whenever its directions do not follow the building, so the exposure and Kzt of each face, the terrain profiles and the report
  pages use the building's own directions (e.g. ridge at 053 deg: 008, 053, 098 ... deg).
Tests: WindAuditTests 79, WindBuildingLoadsTests 42, WindCasesTabTests 9.
