DTCE Tool Kit v1.5.2 test build (18 November 2026) - bug fix

Bill of Quantities include checkbox (owner: "kapag in-uncheck ko yung checkbox sa
BOQ, hindi siya ma-unmap; stay pa din doon, di lang masasama sa total")
- An unchecked quantity stays mapped and listed under its pay item with its
  own quantity (shown struck through); only the pay-item total leaves it out.
  Before, the row showed 0 and looked removed.
- Unchecking now also works for automatically suggested mappings (before, it
  only changed the total for mappings already saved in the workbook).
- "Leave unmapped" (right-click) is the separate action that removes the
  mapping; it also clears the unchecked state.

Includes v1.5.1. Not a Microsoft Store release (v1.5.0 is the Store build).
