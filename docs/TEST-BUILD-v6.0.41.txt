DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.41 test build (21 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (Bill of Quantities):
- Dropdowns and checkboxes in every Bill of Quantities sheet apply at once (choosing Rebar layers 1 / 2 used to change nothing
  until the cell was left).
- RC Wall Estimate: side B of a two-layer wall can have its own bars - new columns Side B vertical dia / spacing and Side B horizontal
  dia / spacing (blank = as side A; a blank spacing takes side A's spacing). The rebar lines read "side A" / "side B". The side B
  columns are grey and locked until Rebar layers = 2. Saved with the project; older projects open as before.
- Checkbox first: Roofing "Insulation?" comes before "Insulation type", which is grey and locked until Insulation? is ticked.
- Insulation rolls default to 1 m wide x 50 m long (blank values in older projects are filled with 1 x 50).
- Earthworks Start uses the whole window: the settings in one band across the top, the earthworks summary and the gravel quantities
  side by side under it, filling the height.
- Sheet guides: shown by the info icon at the upper right of the window for the open tab; the second small icon in each sheet
  heading is removed.
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Tests: OpeningLintelTests (21: lintels, RC wall side B, insulation defaults), UpdateAndQuantityTests.

Includes v6.0.40 and earlier builds.
