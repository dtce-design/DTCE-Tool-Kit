PATCH: extends the printed Bar Bending Schedule (beams) for long beam lines.

Long beam lines ("Pano if 10-20 spans ang beam")
- The elevation of a beam line is drawn in strips of whole spans of about 21 m or less, so it stays near 1:55 on A3 (a 15-span
  line was 1:272 and unreadable). Each strip shows its spans ("SPANS 4 TO 6 OF 15"), its supports, bars running through with
  their marks, laps, hoop zones, section cuts and dimensions (S4 to S7 length and the line overall). Strips meet at MATCH LINE
  marks at the support centre.
- Identical sections (same beam, bars, hoops and position) are drawn once as "SECTION 2B (TYP.)" with the cuts they stand for,
  e.g. "Typ. at 2B to 14B" (45 cuts of a 15-span line become 4 sections).
- Extra hoops over laps get their bar marks after the schedule stirrups, so the continuous bars keep consecutive marks.

Includes v1.49.10 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
