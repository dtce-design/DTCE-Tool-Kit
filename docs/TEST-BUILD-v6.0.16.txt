DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.16 test build (20 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (one look for the workspaces; owner chose "lahat ganyan" and a navy header):
- Workspace header in navy (matches the folder tabs) with the white title and the info icon at the upper right.
- Every design sheet page has the same two panes: on the left a compact white input form - one row per input, the label beside
  its box, numbers right-aligned; on the right the section previews, tables, action rows and their headings. A page without such
  blocks shows the calculation report on the right, updated from the inputs; a page without inputs uses the full width. Opening
  the Calculation report tab fills the report.
- Bar Bending Schedule: settings in a left pane (label beside each input); beam lines and the results on the right.
- Purlin Design keeps its own page (form left; result band, section, properties and checks right).
Checked on Linux/Mono only (screens of every design sheet, the Bar Bending Schedule, Project Information and the formatters).
