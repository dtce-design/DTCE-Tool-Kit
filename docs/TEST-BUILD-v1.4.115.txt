DTCE Tool Kit v1.4.115 (18 November 2026)

Builds on v1.4.114.
- Owner: INCLUDE column is only as wide as its checkbox (fixed 76 px).
- Owner: new STATUS column on the right of Bill of Quantities. Pay item: "Linked: n mapped[, m excluded]",
  "Manual quantity", "Excel value". Mapped quantity: "Mapped", "Auto-matched", "... · excluded", "Manual quantity",
  "Automatic 1 LS / 0 LS". Unmapped quantity: "Not mapped".
- Owner: mappings can be changed in Bill of Quantities. Click the (blue) STATUS of a quantity to choose another
  BACKUP item in the searchable picker; right-click for Change / Leave unmapped / Include in total. The choice is
  saved with the project (BackupBillRoutes/Override) and wins over the workbook mapping on the next Excel update;
  the Map BACKUP items dialog remains authoritative when used.
- Owner: Bill of Quantities grouped by PART following the owner's BACKUP layout: A Facilities for the Engineer,
  B Other General Requirements, C Pavement, D Slope Protection, E Earthworks, F Ground Improvement, G Piling,
  H Reinforced Concrete, I Finishing Works with I.1 Termite Control ... I.8 Roofing, J Plumbing / Sanitary,
  K Electrical, L Mechanical, M Water Supply, N Water Pumping (part from the item number).
- Painting items are not shown as auto-matched in the app BOQ either (consistent with the Excel write).
- Mono checks: statuses shown; remap to another LS item and "leave unmapped" work and are saved; PART headings
  appear in the owner's order A..N.
