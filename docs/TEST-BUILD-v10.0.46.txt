PATCH - roof live load at every level of the model ("madetermine niya sana yung mga ibat ibang levels based din sa model"):
- The LLR case finds the roofs at every level of the pasted STAAD model by itself:
  slab roofs - every panel of every level that no higher level covers (main roof, lower roofs at setbacks, stair / penthouse roofs);
  trussed roofs - the trusses rising from any level (over the top floor, over a lower wing, a canopy), not only over the top floor.
- Stairs and ramps are not roofs: a group of connected sloped members that reaches a higher floor (a joint on that floor's outline or
  edge) is left out; beams of a higher floor are not taken as truss members.
- The ceiling under each trussed roof takes no roof load, at whatever level it is.
- Each roof is named with its level in the STAAD comments and in the report ("level 4 m, truss Z = 6 m"; "level 8 m").
Tests: RoofLiveLoadsTests 18 (a lower wing with trusses at 4 m beside a two-storey block with a stair from 4 to 8 m),
WindEdgeLoadsTests 42, WindEdgeShapesTests 23, WindCasesTabTests 41.
