DTCE Tool Kit v1.15.0 test build (18 November 2026) - behaviour change

Door & Window Schedule (owner: "i-combine na lang yung paint ng leaf at jamb")
- One checkbox, "Paint (leaf and jamb)", now controls painting of both the
  wooden leaf and its jamb. The separate "Paint jamb" column is removed.
- Unticked: no leaf or jamb paint, and Paint faces is not required (v1.14.1).
- Older projects open with the combined box following their saved leaf setting.

Includes v1.14.1. Not a Microsoft Store release (v1.5.0 is the Store build).
