PATCH: Strap Foundation - do the bars fit in one layer.

- Strap bars ("icheck mo din sa strap foundation if kasya naman yung no. of bars sa isang layer"): new checks "Strap top bars fit in
  one layer" and "Strap bottom bars fit in one layer" (NSCP 425.2.1). Width inside the stirrups = bw - 2 cover - 2 stirrup Ø; clear
  spacing >= max(25 mm, db, 4/3 aggregate) gives the most bars per layer; the number of bars is checked against it. When they do not
  fit, the check fails and the steps say how many layers they need (for example 6 + 2) with Ø25 spacers, or to widen the strap /
  use bigger bars. Example: 400 mm strap, 40 cover, Ø10 stirrups -> 300 mm; Ø25 bars -> 6 per layer: 4-25 fits, 8-25 does not.
- Footing bars: new check "Footing bars fit in one layer (clear spacing)": s - db >= max(25 mm, db, 4/3 aggregate).

Includes v5.1.5 and earlier builds (see their notes).
