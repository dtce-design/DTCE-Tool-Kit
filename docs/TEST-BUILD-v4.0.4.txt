PATCH: extends the existing Truss Support on RC design sheet and Bar Bending Schedule workspace.

("May model sana para sa truss anchorage para ma visualization", "Ganon din sana sa BBS may visualization",
"Iscale naman yung drawing")
Truss Support on RC
- New 3D view beside the plan and elevation: RC support (see-through), base plate, gusset, anchor bolts with nuts and
  hooks; drag to turn it, double-click to reset; failing parts are red as in the other views.
- Plan and elevation are drawn at one standard scale (1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:15, 1:20 ... the largest that fits),
  shown with a scale bar; the plate thickness is drawn to scale too.
Bar Bending Schedule
- Model: "True scale" (on by default) draws the beam depth, cover, bar positions, hooks and laps at the same scale as
  the length; zoom in to read the detail, drag up / down / left / right to pan. The current scale is shown (1:n on
  screen). Untick it for the earlier exaggerated depth.
- Bar bending schedule tab: a SKETCH column with the shape of every bar, and a to-scale drawing of the selected bar on
  the right with A / B / C, cut length, pieces and weight.

Includes v4.0.3 (was v1.49.3) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
