PATCH: extends the existing Truss Support on RC design sheet (owner's numbering rule).

Truss Support on RC - designs supports like the owner's Detail 6 ("Yung kaya magdesign ng ganitong support")
- "Typical detail" button fills Detail 6: 250 x 300 x 16 mm base plate (250 along the truss), 2-20 mm L anchor bolts
  each side of the truss at 50 mm from the plate edges and 90 mm apart (300 mm long: hef 240, hook 80), 6 mm gusset
  320 x 250, on a 300 x 300 RC column; every value stays editable.
- A gusset longer than the plate is allowed; it is welded to the plate (and checked in shear) over the plate length only.
- Other members at the joint (bottom chord, vertical post, diagonal): a table with name, 2L / 1L, angle section, STAAD
  axial force (+ tension / - compression), heel / toe weld lengths and weld size. Each member gets its weld, angle and
  gusset base metal, tension yielding / rupture (tension) or Whitmore buckling (compression) and Whitmore yielding checks,
  with the balanced heel / toe weld lengths, in Results and in the report. Saved with the sheet.
- Plan and elevation drawing of the support from the inputs (plate, RC support, gusset, anchor positions and hooks,
  dimensions), updated as you type.

Includes v1.49.0 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
