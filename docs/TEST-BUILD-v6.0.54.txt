PATCH (Bar Bending Schedule; owner: "if same lang naman length ng bar marks at termination niya bat hindi na lang same bar mark?"):
- Identical bars of a beam line share one bar mark: same face (top / bottom / stirrup / side face / spacer), diameter, grade,
  shape and ends, cut length after rounding and, for hoops, the same section. E.g. every 2L-Ø10 hoop of a 300x500 line is one
  S mark; identical extra bars at different supports are one T or B mark. The schedule per element lists each mark once with
  the bar numbers summed; the elevation and bar details still show each placement with its count.
- A bar removed on the cutting list keeps a mark of its own.
Checked on Linux/Mono only.

Includes v6.0.53 and earlier builds.
