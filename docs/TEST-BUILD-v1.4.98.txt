  offers the full handbook tables: heavy gauge ERW pipe (printed p.15), light gauge TRI-R pipe (p.16), Strux

- The handbook has no plain round bar or square bar; those stay computed from 7850 kg/m3 and are labelled so.
- Railing sizes saved by v1.4.96 (e.g. "Pipe OD33.4x3.4mm", family "Pipe") still open and compute.
- Checked on Linux/Mono: RailingTests (22 checks, handbook values per table) and a POW run with heavy and
