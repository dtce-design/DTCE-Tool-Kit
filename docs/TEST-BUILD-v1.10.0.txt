DTCE Tool Kit v1.10.0 test build (18 November 2026)

Railing painting per member (owner: "lagyan ng check box sa railing if with
painting yung member or wala")
- New "With painting?" checkbox on every Railing Estimate row (checked for
  new rows). Unchecked members get 0 m2 painting and are left out of
  "Railing metal painting - installed" and its breakdown; steel pieces and
  weights are unchanged.
- Projects saved before this version open with every railing row checked
  (as they were all painted before).

Includes v1.9.0. Not a Microsoft Store release (v1.5.0 is the Store build).

Metal painting broken down (owner: "sa metal painting i-breakdown mo yung sa
purlin, angle bar, sagrod at cross bracing")
- Bill of Quantities now lists metal painting per member type instead of one
  total: "Metal painting - purlins", "- angle bars", "- metal fascia",
  "- crossbracing rods" (each with its members), plus the existing "Sag rod
  metal painting" and "Railing metal painting" lines.
- A project that had mapped the old "Metal painting TOTAL" line keeps that
  pay item for the new lines automatically. The total stays in the steel
  summary and reports for reference.
