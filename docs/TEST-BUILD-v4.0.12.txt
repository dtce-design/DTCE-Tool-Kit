PATCH: extends the Bar Bending Schedule workspace (beams) with crew site sheets.

Site sheets for the rebar crew ("Wala na bang masmaganda at mas maayos na presentation para magets ng mga construction workers?")
- New button "Print site sheets (crew)": an A3 landscape packet, separate from the engineering BBS, labelled in English and
  Filipino:
  - HOW TO READ / PAANO BASAHIN: units (mm), the three steps, and one colour per bar size (Ø10 grey, Ø12 orange, Ø16 blue,
    Ø20 red, Ø25 green ...), used on every sheet.
  - CUTTING / PAGPUTOL: one line per cutting pattern - the commercial bar drawn to scale with its cut pieces and lengths,
    red cut marks, the offcut hatched (KEEP / ITABI when 1 m or more), "x N" bars to cut that way in big type, the bar marks
    the pieces are for, and boxes to tick per bar done.
  - BENDING / PAGBALUKTOT: one card per bar mark - mark and size on a coloured band, quantity in big type (per beam x beams),
    cut length, the shape drawn large with the length of every leg written on it (hooked bars drawn with a shortened
    straight part; the written lengths govern), where it goes, and a done box.
  - PLACING / PAGLALAGAY: the elevation strips and sections of the BBS.
  - Warning line when the BBS has open review items, and blank lines for release by the engineer, the foreman and the site
    checker.

Includes v4.0.11 (was v1.49.11) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
