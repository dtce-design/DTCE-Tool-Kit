DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.27 test build (20 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH:
- Manual column schedule in the Bill of Quantities, per level range ("manual sched sa boq hindi sa bbs"; "sa column dapat per
  elevation siya kasi baka magbago rebar per height"): the Manual column schedules (Reinforced Concrete Works > Column Estimate)
  have From level (m) and To level (m). A mark may have one row per level range (e.g. C1 0.00-3.50 8-Ø20, C1 3.50-7.00 8-Ø16). A
  Column Estimate row with that mark takes every range: its height is the full height, and each range is taken off with its own
  section, bars and ties; the bars of a range lap into the range above with the compression lap (NSCP 425.5.5.1). Marks without
  levels, and projects saved before, work as before. The Bar Bending Schedule takes these columns with Load from Bill of
  Quantities, one bar mark per range.
- The column / footing schedules added to the Bar Bending Schedule in v6.0.26 are removed (they belong in the Bill of Quantities).
- Cut bars and the beam estimate curtailment ("yung cut bars diba may curtailment setting"): the Bar Bending Schedule already used
  the same curtailment as the beam estimate (top L/4 at end and continuous supports; bottom midspan bars from L/6; bottom at supports
  L/4); top bars in the middle only (more top bars at midspan than at the supports) were missing - they are now the central L/2 plus
  the cut-end allowance, as in the beam estimate.
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Tests: BeamCuttingListTests (90), MemberSchedulesTests (29, with the level-range checks),
MemberBbsTests, EstimatorRowIncludeTests.

Includes v6.0.26 and earlier builds.
