DTCE Tool Kit v7.1.11 (23 November 2026) - test build; not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v7.1.7 is the requested Store release).

PATCH - Bill of Quantities linked to the POW BACKUP:
- Progress Photographs follow the Contract Duration of the Bill of Quantities (owner: "progress photo graph naka link sa contract
  duration ng boq"): months = contract duration (calendar days) / 30, rounded up, shown as filled by the app and still editable;
  a quantity typed in the app wins. With no contract duration yet (empty construction schedule) the quantity already in the linked
  BACKUP is kept. This replaces v7.1.10 (Progress Photographs read from the workbook).
- Typed values in the linked BACKUP are overridden even when the app has no quantity (owner: "pati yung mga may type values sa excel
  ioverride niya din kahit walang nakalagay sa app"): a typed BACKUP!BC value of a mapped pay item without an app quantity is
  cleared (formula cells stay; the Progress Photographs row is not cleared). The status bar shows how many were cleared. The same
  applies to the built-in POW workbook BACKUP.
Not verified here: the Excel (COM) clearing runs only on Windows with Excel; check it on a copy of your workbook.
