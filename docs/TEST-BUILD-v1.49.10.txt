PATCH: fixes the lap splices of the Bar Bending Schedule (beams).

Lap locations reviewed ("Sure ka sa mga lap location?")
- Special moment frame: laps stay out of the joints and 2h from each face (NSCP 418.6.3.3). Within that, top bars are now
  lapped about midspan and bottom bars just outside 2h, away from the midspan positive moment (before, a lap could fall
  wherever the 12 m bar ended, e.g. a bottom lap at midspan). The farthest preferred position within reach of the bar is
  used; only when none fits is the lap put at the farthest allowed point and flagged in the splice check.
- Splice laps are Class B (1.3 ld, NSCP 425.5.2) because all bars of a group are lapped at one place; before, the class of
  the Splice, Development & Hooks configuration was used. The POW "1 x lap" piece allowance is unchanged.
- Hoops over each lap at min(d/4, 100 mm) are now added to the schedule ("Extra hoops over lap" bars, in the estimate of
  their span) where the schedule hoops are wider, instead of only reporting NG. They are drawn in the elevation.
- Fewer long pieces may become one more short piece because of the preferred positions; the cutting patterns follow.

Includes v1.49.9 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
