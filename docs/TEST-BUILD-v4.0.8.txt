PATCH: extends the existing Bar Bending Schedule workspace (beams).

Standard presentation of the BBS and cutting list ("Standard presentation ng bbs at cutting list")
- "Print bar bending schedule" now prints a presentable A4 document: 1 Schedule information (BBS no., revision, status,
  materials, laps/hooks configuration, cover, frame, curtailment, commercial lengths), 2 Shape key with drawn shapes,
  3 Bar bending schedule (member, bar mark, type & size, no. of members, no. in each, total no., length, shape code,
  sketch of each bar, A / B / C, weight), 4 Weight summary per grade and size, 5 Cutting list with the cutting pattern of
  every commercial bar and the order (bars to buy per length, bought / cut length, offcut %, weight bought),
  6 SMF detailing check, 7 Splice zone check, review items and notes, blank signature lines.
- Shape codes of the schedule: S0 straight, L1 90° hook one end, U2 90° hooks both ends, H5 closed hoop with 135° hooks,
  X3 crosstie 135° / 90°. They are this schedule's codes (sketch and A / B / C govern), not BS 8666 / CRSI numbers.
- New result tab "Cutting patterns": pieces of the same grade and size are packed into commercial bars (best-fit
  decreasing, each bar bought at the shortest length that holds its cuts); identical bars are one pattern, with the bar
  marks it serves; offcuts of 1.0 m or more are marked (keep).

Special moment frame detailing per NSCP 2015 ("gamit ang smf kasi yun lang ang permitted sa ph")
- New result tab "SMF detailing check" (special moment frame is the default): per span Ln >= 4d and bw >= min(0.3h, 250)
  (418.6.2.1); As >= As,min at every section, ρ <= 0.025 and at least 2 continuous bars top and bottom (418.6.3.1);
  M+ at the face >= ½ M- and any section >= ¼ the face maximum, through the steel areas (418.6.3.2); first hoop <= 50 mm,
  hoops over 2h at <= min(d/4, 6db, 150) (418.6.4.1 / .4), d/2 elsewhere (418.6.4.6); hoop legs <= 350 mm apart;
  135° hook extension >= max(6db, 75) (425.3.2); laps outside joints and 2h with hoops at <= min(d/4, 100) (418.6.3.3);
  interior joints: column depth >= 20 db (418.8.2.3); exterior joints: ldh = max(8db, 150, fy db / 5.4√fc') (418.8.5.1).
- Not checked here (structural design): bw against the column width (418.6.2.1(c)), joint shear, strong column - weak
  beam and the shear design of the hoops. Failing items are listed in Review and in the printed schedule.
