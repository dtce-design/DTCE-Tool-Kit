DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.13 test build (20 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (Purlin Design, info buttons):
- Purlin Design ("alisin na yung section properties sa purlin design na tab, icompute na lang internally"; "masmagandang
  workspace"): the Section properties tab and the "use verified properties" inputs are removed; the properties are always
  computed from the Regan handbook profile (rectangular plates, bend radii neglected, Ixy kept for Z shapes) and shown read-only
  under the section preview. The inputs are on one page in groups: MEMBER AND SECTION, GEOMETRY, LOADS, MATERIAL. Purlin files
  saved before this build (with the Ix, Iy, Ixy, Sx, Sy and Verified inputs) still open; those values are ignored.
- Info button ("pagparehasin, icon na lang siya, isang location na lang sa upper right, alisin mo yung DTCE / ENGINEERING";
  "nagpapalit yung info button based sa tab na nakaopen"): every window has one info icon (ⓘ) at the upper right. It shows the
  note of the tab that is open (the innermost open tab with a note) and is greyed out on tabs without one. It replaces the
  "DTCE / ENGINEERING" caption in the workspace header, the Info buttons on design-sheet tabs and the Cutting List beam lines,
  and the "?" button of the Construction Schedule.
Checked on Linux/Mono only (purlin round trip, older purlin file, info note per tab). Tests: SteelSectionRefreshUiTests,
BeamCuttingListTests. SteelFeatureTests stops on Mono in the estimator purchase totals (also without these changes); Windows CI
runs it.

Includes v6.0.12 and earlier builds.
