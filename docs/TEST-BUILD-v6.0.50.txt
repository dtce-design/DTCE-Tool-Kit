DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.50 test build (22 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v6.0.48 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (Bar Bending Schedule, beam):
- Model view: a distinct separator - the elevation stops before the section panel, which has its own shaded background and a
  solid divider.
- Sections (model view and BBS report): show the bars that really cross the cut, the same bars as the elevation (extra bars
  that run past the zone were missing). Two pieces of one bar lapping at the cut count once. Layer 1 as scheduled, the rest in
  the next layers. When the schedule count of the zone differs, the model view says so.
- Curtailment: extra bars past an interior support now follow the curtailment too - L/4 of the neighbouring span, not less
  than Ld from the face where the bar is needed (they ran a full Ld past the far face of the support before).
- Model view: curtailment dimensions for every extra bar (L/4, L/3, L/6 from the face, and any cut-end allowance past it).
- Extra hoops over laps: laps that overlap in one span share one evenly spaced set of hoops (overlapping laps doubled the
  hoops and drew them unevenly).
- Layer spacer bars (Ø25, Grade 40) are drawn end-on between the layers in the model view and in the report elevation.
- Report elevation: the depth is at the same scale as the length, enlarged only when the bar layers would not fit (the caption
  says by how much).
- Shape key: the hoop has longer, separated 135° hooks, C (hook length) labelled on the hook and 135° shown; each shape in its own
  framed cell with its name; schedule information in two item/value pairs per row (easier to read printed on A3).
- Bar codes spaced: "T1 - 4 NOS. Ø16,  L = 11750", stirrups "S17 - 10 NOS. Ø10 @ 100 (2L)".
Checked on Linux/Mono only.

Includes v6.0.49 and earlier builds.
