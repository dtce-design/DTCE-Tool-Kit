PATCH: Bar Bending Schedule (beams) - layer spacer bars.

Layer spacer bars ("Sa BBS sinama mo ba yung bar spacer? If may 2 layers ganon. Kasi 25mm yun e")
- Where the bars of a face do not fit one layer (clear spacing max(25 mm, db, 4/3 aggregate), NSCP 425.2.1), they go in two or
  more layers and Ø25 spacer bars are added across the beam between the layers (NSCP 425.2.2: layers at least 25 mm clear, the
  upper bars directly above). Spacers are placed over the length where the extra layer exists, at the set spacing (default
  1000 mm, at least two per stretch), one set per gap between layers; length = beam width - 2 covers - 2 hoop diameters.
  Lapped pieces of one bar count once (contact laps side by side).
- New settings: layer spacer bar Ø (default 25 mm) and spacer spacing (default 1000 mm); saved with the cutting list.
- Spacers appear in the bar bending schedule, cutting patterns, estimate, crew site sheets, the elevation (brown dots) and the
  sections (bars in layers with the spacer drawn between them). The SMF check uses d for two layers.
