DTCE Tool Kit v1.4.129 test build (18 November 2026)

Angle bars (owner: "sa truss profile bat walang unequal angle bar?")
- Added the unequal-leg angle bars from the Regan 2026 handbook p.9
  (printed p.04, JIS G3192:2008): 75x50, 90x75, 100x75, 125x75, 125x90,
  150x75, 150x90 and 150x100, 33 sizes, named e.g. "L100x75x8mm (JIS unequal)".
  H = long leg A, B = short leg B; masses as published.
- Also added the missing equal-leg sizes from handbook p.8 (printed p.03):
  100x100x9/10/12, 125x125, 150x150, 200x200 and 250x250 (14 sizes).
- They appear wherever angle bars are listed: Truss connection profile,
  Structural Steel angle bars, fascia frames and painting areas.
- Truss connection area and centroid offset use both legs (outstanding leg
  = B). Verify the connected leg for unequal angles in the design.
