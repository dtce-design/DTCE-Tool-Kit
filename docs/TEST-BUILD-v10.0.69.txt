PATCH - Design Loads / Load Combinations (owner, 28 November 2026: "huwag gawin automatic"; "yung no. of wind load cases lang ang automatic,
pero yung eq ll, llr automatic pa din ... yung sa eq ako magseselect if anong case sa primary cases"; "bakit yung LLR nasa load combo report?
diba dapat separate na tab ang LLR?"; "naimomodel ng roof kahit wala naman beam doon na parte sa gitna na nagdudugtong"):
- EQ primary cases are chosen on the Primary loads tab ("EQ primary cases": C orthogonal / B accidental torsion / A each direction alone; the
  same choice as in Settings, kept in step). The irregularity check no longer changes it; it shows its suggestion under the choice and the
  "Use ..." button on the Structural irregularities tab stays. The wind load cases, LL1 / LL2 and LLR stay automatic.
- "Roof live load (LLR)" is its own Design Loads sub tab (after Live Loads) and its own part of the Design Loads calculation report
  ("Roof Live Load (LLR) - NSCP 2015"); it is no longer a section of the Load Combinations report.
- Roof FLOOR LOAD ranges never bridge a gap without beams: a range holds only the cells of its own panels (never an open panel inside the
  outline, nor ground between two separate roofs). Two rooftop structures with no beam between them are loaded and drawn apart. The same
  rule applies to the wind roof FLOOR LOADs.
