PATCH: wall finishes without a Wall ID come off plaster and masonry paint.

- "Iminus na lang sa plaster at paint yung mga wall tiles and finishes?" (owner: "check mo if ano ang maganda; sa lumang set up pwede
  kasing madaming wall ID sa isang wall finish"). Wall finish rows no longer carry a Wall ID (v5.1.3), so their area is taken off the
  project totals:
  - Tiles and Other finishes: off the net plaster and off the masonry paint (as tiles linked to a wall always were);
  - Paint finishes: off the masonry paint only (that row is the paint; the plaster stays under it).
  The deduction is shared over the walls in proportion to their plaster / paint area, so each wall in the Bill of Quantities stays
  positive and the totals are right; the cells show the share in their tooltip. If the finishes are larger than the plaster or
  paint, that total is 0 and a review note is shown.
- Rows that still have a Wall ID (older projects) keep their per-wall deduction as before.

Includes v5.1.3 and earlier builds (see their notes).
