Changes in this draft
- RC Start: remove redundant stock-length checklists. Beam/slab selectors stay
  in their existing member settings; add commercial-length picker to footing
  settings. Misc/slope/wall-footing stock picker is inside member settings.
- RC Start rebuilt into compact Member Settings, Cover/Foundation Defaults,
  and Concrete Supply cards. Lean numeric input stays128px; field rows wrap.
  Remove the old resize handler that stretched every child to screen width.
- Main/sub/nested tab levels have distinct dark strip shades, with full
  caption measurement and native wrapping/hit-testing. A NavigationTabs
  subclass paints unused strip background to avoid bright native tab space.
- Beam summary no longer occupies a narrow right pane: Selected Beam Details,
  By Diameter and Grade40/Grade60 use full-width bottom tabs. Summary columns
  have minimum widths; beam entry headers have more room.
- Clear vertical and horizontal table dividers; measured minimum header-word
  widths, wrapped headers and a subdued input/calculated surface distinction.
- Formatter tabs open their tool directly. Remove Open Beam Formatter/Open
  Slab Formatter intermediate buttons. Existing per-program checks retained.
- POW construction batches form/tab layout. BOQ rows are prepared and added
  in one batch; auto row sizing pauses until population completes. A fresh,
  empty catalogue skips building unused calculation demands. Stored workbook
  and manual quantities bypass that empty-catalogue path.
