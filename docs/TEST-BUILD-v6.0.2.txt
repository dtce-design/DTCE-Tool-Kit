DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.2 test build (20 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (Program of Works (POW) workspace): BACKUP is linked to the Bill of Quantities workspace ("yung backup sa pow nakalink sa
boq workspace").
- "BACKUP linked to Bill of Quantities" (on by default): whenever the POW window comes to the front, or the POW opens, the quantity of
  every pay item exactly as the Bill of Quantities shows it (computed take-off, typed Part A / B and manual quantities) is written to
  BACKUP column BC of its row; the row is matched by the BACKUP row and item no. (an item whose row differs is found by its item no.).
  Item rows with no quantity in the Bill of Quantities are cleared, so DETAILED, ABC, SUM, BOQ and SOQ show only the linked items
  (rows without quantity are hidden as X). Formula quantity cells (e.g. A.1.4(1) months) are left alone.
- "Update BACKUP from Bill of Quantities" applies the link on demand; the status bar lists items not found in BACKUP.
- The linked quantities are saved with the POW project (.pow.xml) like any typed value.
- The Bill of Quantities workspace must be open (with its .boq.xml) for the link to read it.
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Test: PowWorkbookEngineTests (45 checks, incl. the link on the built-in V6.02 template).

Includes v6.0.1 and earlier builds.
