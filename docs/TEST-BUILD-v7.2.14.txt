din sa report"; "override Mmax based on Geotechnical Report"):
- New input section "Static lateral force procedure" (NSCP 2015 Section 208.5.2): structural system for Ct (steel MRF 0.0853, RC MRF /
  EBF 0.0731, others 0.0488), storey levels "height m @ seismic weight kN", Ω0, ρ, regularity, optional dynamic base shear from STAAD.
  The summary under the inputs shows T, W, V (and V/W), Ft and the dynamic scale factor.
- Report section 6 "Static lateral force procedure" (src/StaticLateralForce.cs): parameter table (Z, source / distance, Na / Nv,
  Ca / Cv, I, R, Ω0, ρ, Ct, hn, W, regularity) and the calculation sheet: T = Ct hn^(3/4) (208-12); V = Cv I W / (R T) (208-8);
  limits 2.5 Ca I W / R (208-9), 0.11 Ca I W (208-10), 0.8 Z Nv I W / R in Zone 4 (208-11) with the governing equation named;
  Ft = 0.07 T V <= 0.25 V (0 for T <= 0.7 s); Σ wi hi; Fx = (V - Ft) wx hx / Σ wi hi (Section 208.5.5); table of storey forces,
  storey shears and overturning moments; Figure 5 storey forces and shear diagram; Eh, Ev = 0.5 Ca I D, E = ρ Eh + Ev (208-1),
  Em = Ω0 Eh (208-2); minimum dynamic base shear (90% regular / 100% irregular, 208.5.4.5) and the STAAD scale factor.
  Later sections renumbered (7 STAAD scale ... 10 notes).
- Mmax from the geotechnical report: new inputs "Mmax from the geotechnical report" and the report reference. When entered, it
  overrides the automatic Mmax (fault data, picked fault, rupture length) and sets the source type; the report cites it.
