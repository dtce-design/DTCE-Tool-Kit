PATCH: Finish Works totals per group in BOQ style.

- TOTALS PER GROUP ("parang boq style na lang na may drop down arrow ilagay doon yung mga kasama sa group"): each BOQ group
  is a bold row with an arrow in the row header. Click the arrow (or the group name, or double-click the row) to show the
  measured entries of that group below it: entry ID and location, Finish ID, description and area (m2). Click again to
  hide them. Open groups stay open when the sheet refreshes. A closed group still lists its entries in one line.

Includes v1.50.10 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
