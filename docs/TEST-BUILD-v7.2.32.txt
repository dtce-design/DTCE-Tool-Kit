PATCH - Wind Load topographic factor Kzt, correction of v7.2.31:
- v7.2.31 set every water cell (land cover D) to 0 m. That is right for the sea but wrong for inland lakes and rivers: a lake at
  700 m would have become 0 m and looked like a 700 m escarpment.
- Now only elevations below 0 m (sea floor in the elevation data, values blended at the shore) are taken at mean sea level, 0 m.
  Inland lakes and rivers keep their surface elevation from the data. The check row is "Sea surface - 0 m at n point(s)".
Tests: WindAuditTests 79, WindRecoveryTests 40.
Includes everything of v7.2.31.
