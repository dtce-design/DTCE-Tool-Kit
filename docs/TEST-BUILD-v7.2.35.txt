PATCH - Beam Formatter, ACI schedule option (owner: "sa bar placing diagram meron din ba ng diagram if may bottom bars sa support?";
"ano ba yung nasa aci manual may G?"; "lahat ng pwedeng mangyari sa schedule ilagay mo"):
- New schedule column G under BOTTOM: added bottom bars at the supports ("L: ..." left, "R: ..." right, or one value when both supports
  have the same). They were only a remark ("BOT. LEFT +...") before. G is not a letter of ACI MNL-66 Fig. BM-1_1; it is added for this
  schedule and the notes say so.
- New placing diagram "BOTTOM BARS AT THE SUPPORTS, G" (when a beam has G): at an interior support one straight bar through the column,
  Ln/4 past each face (not less than ld); at an end support a standard hook in the column and Ln/4 into the span - as the Bar Bending
  Schedule cuts them.
- New placing diagram "CANTILEVER" (when a beam is cantilevered): top bars to the free end with a standard hook down and into the back
  span (by the design, not less than ld, commonly not less than the cantilever length), bottom bars of the back span and of the
  cantilever, ties to the free end. Note: the Bar Bending Schedule does not cut cantilever spans.
- Notes 5 (G) and 6 (cantilever) are added when they apply; long notes no longer overlap (top-anchored, spaced by their wrapped lines).
- The DXF still reads back into the Bar Bending Schedule and the Bill of Quantities (hidden row data unchanged).
