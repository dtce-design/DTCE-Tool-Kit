PATCH: corrects the existing Truss Support on RC design sheet.

Truss Support on RC - chord on the plate, anchors outside the angles ("Hindi ba yung anchor bolt naka install sa labas ng
angle bar naka weld yung angle bar sa baseplate?")
- New choice "What is welded to the base plate": Bottom chord angles (anchors outside the angles) - the default and the
  Detail 6 / Truss 2 presets - or Gusset plate (the earlier assumption).
- Bottom chord: 1L / 2L angle section, chord-to-plate fillet weld size and length (blank = plate length). Detail 6 uses
  2L50x50x5 and Truss 2 uses 2L75x75x6 as starting sections - change them to the drawing's chord.
- With the chord on the plate: the plate cantilever for bearing is measured from the angle toes ((B - footprint)/2), the
  uplift bending from the angle toe to the anchors; new checks "Chord-to-plate welds" (shear and uplift on the two toe
  welds) and "Chord leg bending - uplift" (horizontal leg from the vertical leg to the toe weld); anchors are refused when
  their nuts would reach the angle legs (minimum gauge shown). The gusset-to-chord welds belong to the truss connection.
- Plan, elevation and 3D views draw the chord angles on the plate with the anchors outside them.
- Default anchor gauge 200 mm and plate width 300 mm for new sheets.

Includes v4.0.4 (was v1.49.4) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
