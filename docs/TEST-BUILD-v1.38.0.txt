Contract Duration - rainy days ("kapag wala pang bagong data ng rainy days, yung luma pa din ang gagawin";

- The rainfall records of the owner's sheet (PAGASA Aparri, Nov 2020 - Aug 2026) are built into the app
  and a new project starts with them, so rainy days are never zero by default.
- Records entered or changed are remembered on this computer; the next new project starts from the
  last records entered.
- Moving average: each month averages its last five recorded years, so a month without the newest
  year's data keeps using the earlier years (e.g. September 2026 not recorded yet: 2021-2025).
