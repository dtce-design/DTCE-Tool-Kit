DTCE Tool Kit v2.1.0 (was v1.45.0) test build (19 November 2026) - not pushed (owner: "huwag mo munang ipush")

Not tested on Windows yet (Linux/Mono checks only). Not a Microsoft Store release.

Beam Deflection Check - Beams to check fill in automatically ("Yung sa beam deflection meron yung window na
automatic na sana", "Yung sa may beams to check")
- Beam lines are suggested as soon as the geometry is loaded - from the .STD model, the .ANL input echo, or the
  Nodes / Beams tables (import or paste) - when the Beams to check list is empty.
- The dead and live service load cases are entered automatically from the STAAD load titles (LOAD n LOADTYPE
  DEAD / LIVE / ROOF LIVE TITLE ...) or from the load case names of the displacement table (DL, SW, SDL, LL, LR,
  ...). Load combinations, wind, seismic and temperature cases are left out.
- The import message boxes are gone: what was imported and what was filled in automatically is shown in the
  Loaded data panel. Review the lines and cases before you calculate.

Includes v2.0.0 (was v1.44.0) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
