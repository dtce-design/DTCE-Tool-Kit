RC Wall "Wall thickness must fit cover..." (owner: "tama naman inputs ko")
- The check itself is correct: with the default 40 mm RC wall cover, a 100 mm
  wall with one layer of 12 mm vertical and 10 mm horizontal bars needs
  2 x 40 + 12 + 10 = 102 mm, 2 mm more than the wall.
- The message now shows the numbers and the fix: set a smaller RC wall cover
  in Reinforced Concrete Works > Start (NSCP 2015 Table 420.6.1.3.1: 20 mm
  for walls not exposed to weather or in contact with ground), then press
  Apply covers to existing entries; or use a thicker wall.
