factor. pero 1 muna ang value, tapos after run papalitan.. include lahat sa report"); src/TorsionAmplification.cs, src/LoadCombinationsAx.cs:
- Ax starts at 1.0 (ACC = 0.05). After the STAAD run, paste the joint displacements of the EQ cases (Node Displacements table or the .ANL
  JOINT DISPLACEMENT block) on the new "Torsional amplification Ax" tab. For each EQ case and floor level, the displacements in the load
  direction at the two extreme edges give dmax and davg; torsional irregularity when dmax > 1.2 davg; Ax = (dmax / 1.2 davg)^2, 1.0 to 3.0.
  The governing Ax replaces the input at once, so ACC = 0.05 Ax in the STAAD primary load section; joints / levels come from the STAAD input
  of the Redundancy factor ρ tab (or an .ANL pasted on the Ax tab).
- Report: its own "Torsional amplification factor Ax" section (formula, table per case and level, governing Ax and ACC), and the basis table
  now lists the primary load mode, seismic load command, torsion of case B (natural + accidental, ACC / Ax) and the seismic drift choice.
