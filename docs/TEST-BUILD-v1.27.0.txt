DTCE Tool Kit v1.27.0 test build (18 November 2026) - new feature

Realistic construction schedule and manpower (owner: "gusto ko yung makatotohanan na
construction schedule at manpower")
- Construction logic instead of whole phases in parallel: each pay item gets a
  trade from its item no. / description and follows the trades it depends on with
  FS / SS / FF relations and lags, e.g. lean concrete overlaps the excavation,
  rebar starts on the lean concrete, formworks overlap the rebar, footing pours
  follow the forms, frame pours finish after the last forms, backfill -> soil
  poisoning -> gravel -> slab on fill, masonry starts with the frame, trusses 7
  working days after the frame (curing), roofing after the steel, insulation and
  accessories with the roofing, plumbing / electrical rough-in before plaster,
  ceiling and tiles, waterproofing before tiles, doors after plaster, painting
  overlapping the plaster and finishing after it. Items of an unknown trade follow
  the work phase of their item no.
- CPM on that network (ES of the first activity = 0, EF = ES + D, LS = LF - D,
  TF = LS - ES). Manpower levelling: non-critical items are started within their
  float on the day that keeps the daily labour lowest; the duration is not changed.
  Lean concrete, footing pours, roof insulation and roofing accessories are not
  moved.
- Manpower: workers = DUPA crew without its foreman x crews; foremen = 1 per 15
  workers (or part) on each day, instead of one foreman for every item.
- PDM: every relation drawn with its label at the arrowhead (e.g. "12 SS +2");
  nodes placed by network level. PERT-CPM: same network (activity on node, since
  activity on arrow cannot show SS / FF overlaps) with a | m | b and ES | te | EF;
  PERT table has a Predecessors column; sigma from the activities that drive the
  finish.
- Sample (42 items): 154 working days (was 226 with whole phases in sequence);
  peak 50 workers (was 110), average 20.4, 3,137 man-days. PERT: P90 163, P95 166.

Includes v1.26.0. Not tested on Windows. Not a Microsoft Store release.
