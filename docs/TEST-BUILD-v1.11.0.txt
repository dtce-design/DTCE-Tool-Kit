DTCE Tool Kit v1.11.0 test build (18 November 2026)

Railings in Bill of Quantities (owner: "tanggalin yung railing weight sa BOQ,
tapos i-group per member type")
- Railing purchase weight and installed weight are no longer BOQ quantities
  (they remain on the Railing Estimate sheet).
- Pieces are grouped per member type: "Railing top rail - pieces to buy
  (6 m stock lengths)", "... mid rail ...", "... bottom rail ...", "... post
  ...", "... baluster ...", "... other ...", each with its profiles and the
  railing rows they cover. Each member type is cut from its own 6 m lengths
  (offcuts reused within that member type).
- Railing metal painting stays (only members ticked "With painting?").

Includes v1.10.0. Not a Microsoft Store release (v1.5.0 is the Store build).
