PATCH: extends the Strap Foundation design sheet; includes v1.50.1 (layer spacer bars in the BBS).

Strap Foundation - NSCP 2015 checks, interaction and visuals ("Full compliant kay NSCP 2015 interactive may visualization pati
report. Yung magandang visuals")
- New checks:
  - punching as a stress with the eccentric shear of the unbalanced moment, vu = Vu/(b0 d) + γv Mu c / Jc (422.6.3.6 /
    408.4.4.2), for interior, edge and corner critical sections (Jc from the actual clipped perimeter);
  - footing depth above the bottom bars >= 150 mm (413.3.1.2); bar spacing <= min(3h, 450) (413.3.2);
  - development of the footing bars from the column face, ld per 425.4.2.3 against the cantilever less end cover (hooks
    noted when short);
  - bearing at the column base, φBn = 0.65 x 0.85 fc' A1 √(A2/A1) <= 2 (422.8.3), dowels 0.005 Ag noted (416.3.4);
  - sliding, μ R / H >= 1.5 with the base friction coefficient (new input, default 0.40);
  - strap: minimum stirrups Av,min (409.6.3.3), stirrup spacing d/4 when Vs > 0.33 √fc' bw d, bars in one layer (clear spacing
    max(25, db, 4/3 aggregate), 425.2.1), skin bars noted when h > 900 (409.7.2.3). New input: maximum aggregate size.
- Interactive model: drag a footing in the plan to move it off its column (ex, ez in 25 mm steps, kept on the footing); click a
  footing to select its row; double-click or "Plan / 3D view" for a shaded 3D view of footings, columns and straps.
- Report: 3D view figure, a footing detail per footing (plan with the bars both ways and the column position, section along X
  with bars, cover and column), the new checks with their derivations.

Includes v5.0.1 (was v1.50.1) and earlier local builds (see their notes).
