DTCE Tool Kit v1.4.65 — canal / septic vault / catch basin in the general items (16 November 2026)

Owner report: quantities of canals, septic vaults and catch basins were not included in the general
concrete, gravel, embankment, excavation, CHB and other items. Confirmed: their materials were only
"basis" breakdown lines under one lump line per structure kind (septic ls, catch basin ea, canal m),
so nothing reached the general items or the Excel BACKUP pay items.

Owner decision: list the structures under each item number; no separate lump line.
- Excavation / embankment: added to the overall earthworks balance (803(1)a, 804(1)a, borrow and
  surplus now include the structures). Sources show e.g. "Septic Vault, Concrete | SV1".
- Gravel: new summary line "Canal / septic vault / catch basin gravel (automatic)"; included in TOTAL
  gravel installed and TOTAL GRAVEL TO BUY (with the gravel purchase allowance) — 804(7).
- Concrete: routed with miscellaneous structures through the concrete pay-item groups (Ready Mix rule).
- Rebar: included in Rebar purchase by size (and Grade 60 routing); cutting plan unchanged.
- Forms 903(2), CHB 1046(2)a1/a2, plaster 1027(1), waterproofing 1016(1)b: new item-numbered rows in
  section "Plumbing Structures", one source line per structure; matched to BACKUP by code/description.
- The septic/catch basin/canal lump lines are no longer in the BOQ or Excel demands. The Plumbing
  Summary screen still shows each structure and its material breakdown for reference. Septic LS input
  is now optional (not used for billing).
- Excel fixed layouts unchanged (WorksSummaryLayout/WorkbookWriter tests pass).
- Note: a project that previously entered a manual LS quantity for a septic vault in the BOQ should
  review that BACKUP row; its materials are now billed through the general items.

Tests: PlumbingRoutingTests 12 (run under Mono/Xvfb with Windows-only calls stubbed), ChamberTakeoff 7,
ProgramOfWorks 14, PowRefinement 28, PowOpenAndIssues 15, WorksSummaryLayout 20, WorksWorkbookWriter 14.
