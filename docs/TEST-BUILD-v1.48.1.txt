Not tested on Windows yet (Linux/Mono checks only). Not a Microsoft Store release.
Version numbering (owner, "Bakit di nasusunod ang version no naming major.minor.bug"; chose "Fixes as PATCH only"): this build
changes an existing workspace, so it raises PATCH. MINOR goes up only for a whole new workspace or design sheet.

Bar Bending Schedule ("Palitan ng Bar Bending Schedule ang Cutting List. Gawin interactive yung model na kayang magmodel yung
cross section ng beam or elevation niya nakikita mo pano yung splicing details niya lahat")
- The Cutting List workspace is now "Bar Bending Schedule" (Workspaces card and window title); its tab is "Beam".
- New Model tab (first): the elevation of the chosen beam line with the supports, spans and clear-span dimensions, the
  allowed splice zones, the stirrup zones with their spacing, every bar on its own lane with hooks, each lap with its
  length (orange; red when the splice check is NG), and section A-A on the right - the beam, slab, stirrup and the top
  and bottom bars at the clicked point with their bar marks, stirrup spacing and any lap at that point.
- Scroll zooms, drag pans, double-click or Fit view fits; hovering a bar shows its mark, location, size, cut length and
  extent; clicking a bar selects it and its rows in the bar bending schedule and the cutting list (and selecting a row
  highlights the bar in the model); clicking the beam moves section A-A.
- Result tabs: Model, Bar bending schedule, Cutting list, Splice zone check, Estimate per span, Commercial bars, Review.

Includes v1.48.0 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
