DTCE Tool Kit v1.11.1 test build (18 November 2026) - formatting

Bill of Quantities readability (owner: "mas maayos na formatting, nakakahilo yung
sub items"; "sa quantity nakakalito"; "dapat naka-tab din sa right")
- Three clear levels:
  1. Pay item (e.g. 903(2) Formworks and Falseworks): bold, quantity at the
     right edge.
  2. Mapped quantity (e.g. Beam forms): semibold on a light band, quantity
     stepped in from the right.
  3. Measurement source (e.g. Beam GB-3 | STAGE): smaller muted text with
     "›", quantity stepped in further.
- Quantities no longer line up as one column of look-alike totals.
