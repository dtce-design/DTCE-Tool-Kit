PATCH (Masonry / Miscellaneous Structures; owner: "Sa calculation ng masonry wall kapag may nailagay ng openings agad na magdagdag ng
lintel beam based dito" - Lintel Beam Design Calculation Report):
- Every opening deducted from a CHB wall (linked door / window / opening schedules and manual openings) gets a lintel automatically,
  per opening and wall, with the opening's units.
- Mark from the report's schedule: CHB thickness (100 mm -> LB-4x, 150 mm and thicker -> LB-6x) and clear opening (0.60-2.00 A,
  2.10-3.00 B, 3.10-4.00 C, 4.10-5.00 D, 5.10-6.00 E; between two ranges the larger mark; under 0.60 m -> A). Openings wider than
  6.00 m are reported for a separate design.
- Take-off: length = clear opening + 2 x bearing (200 / 300 / 400 mm); concrete b x h x L; forms two sides + soffit over the opening;
  bottom / top bars with 90° hooks of 12 db (150 / 200 / 250 / 300 mm), mid bars 2-10 where scheduled, 10 mm ties (single-leg in
  100 mm, closed in 150 mm) at the scheduled spacing, jamb bars 12 mm (1 each side at 200 mm bearing, 2 at 300 / 400 mm) from the
  floor dowel to the hook in the lintel (window head taken at 2.10 m or the opening height). LB-4D / LB-4E note the lateral brace
  (wall vertical bars 10 mm @ 800 hooked into the lintel and dowelled into the beam / slab above).
- Quantities go to Miscellaneous Structures / Lintel Beam (concrete pay item 900(1)i, forms, rebar and commercial-length purchase)
  and are listed in the new tab Miscellaneous Structures > Lintels over Openings, which can be turned off (saved with the project).
  Do not also enter the same lintels in the Lintel Beam sheet.
- Concrete, steel grades and cover follow the Lintel Beam settings (the report uses fc' 21 MPa, Grade 40, 20 mm cover to ties).
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Tests: LintelScheduleTests (21), OpeningLintelTests, OpeningJambAutoTests, UnlinkedFinishTests.

Includes v6.0.31 and earlier builds.
