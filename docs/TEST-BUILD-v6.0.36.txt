PATCH (Bar Bending Schedule):
- Continuous main bars per run of spans: where the top (or bottom) diameter changes along the line (e.g. 3-16 in spans 1 and 2,
  4-20 in span 3) each run gets its own continuous bars - hooked at the beam ends and continued past the support where the diameter
  changes by the curtailment (top L/4 + Ld, bottom Ld). Before, a change of diameter left no continuous bar at all.
- Layer spacer bars are Grade 40 whatever their size (Ø25 spacer = Grade 40), as an exemption from the Grade 60 for 16 mm and up.
- Bar codes use the diameter symbol: T1-8NOS-Ø16, S13-25NOS-Ø10-@100 (2L).
- Shape key and sketches: bends drawn round; R marked at its bend with a short leader; stirrup hooks drawn as two 135° tails into the
  core at one corner, long enough to read.
- Report: the beam elevation and bar details are printed once (section 3); section 4 refers to them instead of repeating them.
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Tests: BeamCuttingListTests (247), MemberBbsTests.

Includes v6.0.35 and earlier builds.
