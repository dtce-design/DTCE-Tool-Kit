PATCH - Wind Load building orientation (owner: "magulo yung ridge alignment? pano ang way para maorient yung building sa windload"):
- The building outline (width x length) is drawn on the site map at the project site, turned to the chosen ridge alignment, with
  the ridge as a red dashed line and the label "Ridge N–S" (etc.). Zoom into the satellite view and pick the alignment whose outline
  lies over the real building. Small buildings are drawn enlarged (marked) so the orientation stays visible.
- The field is labelled "Ridge alignment (compass line of the ridge / long axis; see the outline on the site map)". Older files keep
  their alignment (the choices are unchanged).
