PATCH - Load Combinations: redundancy (reliability) factor ρ computed from the STAAD model (owner: "How do you compute redundancy or
reliability factor"; "Yes with automatic staad computation"). New tab "Redundancy factor ρ" (src/RedundancyFactor.cs,
src/LoadCombinationsRho.cs):
- Paste 1) the STAAD input (.std: UNIT, SET Z UP, JOINT COORDINATES, MEMBER INCIDENCES, simple generation) and 2) the member end
  forces of the EQ load cases (the Beam End Forces table copied from STAAD, or the MEMBER END FORCES block of the .ANL). The EQ cases
  and their directions come from the Primary loads (EQ+X, EQ-Z ...).
- Columns (vertical members), beams (horizontal) and braces (inclined) are found from the geometry; each story shear is the sum of the
  column shears plus the brace horizontal components.
- r_i (NSCP 2015 208.5.1.1): moment frame - the two columns of a bay joined by a beam along the loading direction, 70 % of a column with
  bays on both sides; braced frame - the horizontal component of the most loaded brace; dual system - 80 %; shear walls - typed per story
  (wall shear x 3.05 / lw). Only stories at or below two-thirds of the height count.
- A_B from the base joints (convex hull on plan) or typed. ρ = 2 - 6.1 / (r_max √A_B) per direction, 1.0 ≤ ρ ≤ 1.5, SMRF ≤ 1.25 (NG
  above), Zone 2 = 1.0; the larger direction governs. "Use ρ = ... in the combinations" puts it into Factors.
- The calculation report gets a "Redundancy factor ρ" section (equation, per-direction r_max, story table) when the data were given.
Limits: REPEAT / REPEAT ALL generation in the joint or member list is not read (a note says so); members are classified by geometry,
so a wall modelled with plates needs the typed shear-wall lines.
