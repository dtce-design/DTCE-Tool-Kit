PATCH - Staircase Design supports (owner: "bat ganon yung sa taas"; "siguro pwede mag assign ng beam as support tapos sa location niya sa
landing"):
- New inputs on "Stair type, geometry and model": Supports (Beams / Walls - simple supports), support beam width b and depth h, and
  "Landing beam from the flight (mm, 0 = at the landing end)".
- The section draws the beams where they are: under a landing (flush with its end, or centred at the given distance), and at a flight
  end without a landing the floor beam beyond the last riser with the floor slab - the top of the flight no longer ends in a bare
  riser line. Bottom and top bars run on into the floor beam (top bars hooked down at its far face).
- Analysis: with a landing beam inside the landing, the span ends at the beam and the landing beyond it is an overhang whose hogging
  moment (w a² / 2) is taken over the beam (top bars). The report notes the supports.
- Fix: with "One end continuous" the end moment was applied with the wrong sign (the far end showed sagging instead of hogging, which
  also raised the midspan moment). End moments now vary from the left to the right support value.
