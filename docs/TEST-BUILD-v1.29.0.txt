DTCE Tool Kit v1.29.0 test build (18 November 2026) - new behaviour

Construction Schedule diagrams - continuation sheets (owner: "alisin mo na yung sheet sa
continuation", "baka mas okay yung last box sa previous sheet uulitin sa next page",
"yung madaling maiintindihan yung layout", "huwag isang drawing, masyadong maliit na
yun kapag sa A3")
- The PDM and the PERT-CPM network keep their full, readable size and are printed
  column by column on A3 sheets. Each continuation sheet starts with the last column
  of boxes (PDM) or events (PERT) of the sheet before it, on a light shaded band, and
  the lines carry on from those boxes / events - no "sheet" tags, no match lines, no
  joining of sheets needed. The sheet edges still fall where only straight horizontal
  lines run: no box, event, bend or text is cut by an edge.
- Legends shortened to one line; the definitions (ES/EF/LS/LF/TF, FS/SS/FF and lags,
  milestones; PERT a/m/b, te = m and the +/-20 % note) moved into the node / event key.
- Routing: a relation that cannot get round the labels in a crowded column now at least
  keeps clear of every event.
- The finish collector is reserved before the arrows are routed, and a label crossed by
  a later line is moved along its own arrow, so no line passes under a label.

Construction logic - review of every pay item of the catalogue (owner: "ireview lahat ng
pay items ko para kahit anong pay items man ang maisama tama pa din")
All 268 DUPA items and the 266 Bill of Quantities catalogue items were checked; every
item has a trade except 1003(17) Carpentry and Joinery (follows its work phase).
- Foundations: survey / staking and soil investigation (B.4, B.19) before excavation and
  piling; pile tests 7 days after the first piles and the last pile, footings / pile caps
  after every test; 804(7) gravel bedding under footings and tie beams before the lean
  concrete (the gravel of other areas - slab on fill, walks - is a second bar of the same
  pay item, after the backfill, quantity split in proportion); slab-deck forms and
  falsework separate from the footing forms; the roof deck / roof slab is the last pour.
- Under the slab: sanitary drain, waste and vent piping with the backfill, before soil
  poisoning, gravel bed, vapour barrier and slab on fill; the slab never before the
  footings.
- Superstructure: masonry from 30 % of the frame, the last walls 7 days after the last
  frame pour; door jambs with the masonry; glass blocks after the walls; steel and
  roofing never before the footings / walls in a building without frame concrete; roof
  sheets on painted trusses and purlins; chemical anchors with the steel (cast-in anchor
  bolts with the concrete); storm drainage and downspouts finish after the roofing.
- Finishes: plaster closes the walls after the pipes, conduits, lintels and jambs;
  ceilings after the rough-in and sprinkler piping above them; tiles and cement floor
  finishes 7 days after their slab and under the roof; waterproofing after the plumbing;
  painting 14 days after the plaster and after the roof, finishing after ceilings, tiles
  and doors; wood painting after ceilings, partitions, doors and wood mouldings; metal
  painting with the steel framing, or after doors, grilles and railings when there is no
  steel framing (automotive finish always last); vinyl tiles and epoxy / PU floor
  coatings on a dry slab (24 working days) after the painting; cabinets and countertops
  before the plumbing fixtures; elevators after the hoistway, on permanent power.
- Site and road works: roadway excavation, subgrade, subbase and pavement after the
  building is roofed (on a road job right after clearing); signs, markings and fences
  last; DPWH highway items 100-104, 401-415, 500-511 and 600-699 mapped; 1061(1) Fill
  and Backfill (Pipeline) uses the DUPA output of 1601(1); a water treatment plant is
  equipment, not pipe laying.
- Effect: the sample building now takes 193 working days instead of 154. The painting
  (98 days with one crew) waits for the roof and 14 days of plaster curing; more crews
  for the painting shorten it.
- Summary sheet notes are measured, so a long note no longer runs into the next one.

Includes v1.28.1. Not tested on Windows. Not a Microsoft Store release.
