DTCE Tool Kit v6.0.29 test build (21 November 2026) - not uploaded to the Microsoft Store (v5.1.9 is the last requested upload).

PATCH (Bar Bending Schedule, design sheets):
- BBS model and report: stirrups drawn where they are counted (end groups from each face, middle group evenly between them, none
  doubled up); hoops over a lap drawn in orange at the closer spacing. Slab line removed from the elevation and the sections.
- The bar selected in the elevation is also selected (highlighted, ringed) in Section A-A.
- Report: bar details under each elevation - MAIN TOP STRAIGHT BARS, CUT TOP BARS, MAIN BOTTOM STRAIGHT BARS, CUT BOTTOM BARS - each
  piece with its code (T1-3NOS-T16) and cut length, cranks and hooks, at the elevation's scale; the elevation keeps only the bars.
- Report dimensions: chain of supports and clear spans, chain of 2h zones and the allowed splice zone, overall length, extension
  lines and oblique ticks; section cut names below the beam; elevation height fits its content.
- Report sections: taller boxes so the bar marks under each section are no longer cut off.
- Bar sketches: the crank of K4 / K5 bars is drawn with a visible slope.
- Wording: "stock length(s)" is now "commercial length(s)" everywhere.
- Design sheets: input tabs no longer repeat the calculation report (it has its own tab); tabs without a drawing show their inputs
  in compact columns.
- Strap Foundation: Footings and straps fills the whole page (it was squeezed into the narrow input pane on Windows).
- Wind Load: narrower input column on Building inputs; after Calculate the building shows the pressures - faces coloured, values in
  kPa, rows of pressure arrows (into the face for +, out for -), wind arrow and a list of all surfaces; "Wind from" buttons switch
  the direction (right-click also steps through them).
- Splice, Development & Hooks: same look as the other design sheets (command row on top, white input pane with numbered sections,
  Export menu with PDF and copy).
Checked on Linux/Mono only. Tests: BeamCuttingListTests (242), MemberBbsTests, MemberSchedulesTests, CommercialBarsTests,
StrapFootingTests, WindBuildingLoadsTests, WindAuditTests.

Includes v6.0.28 and earlier builds.
