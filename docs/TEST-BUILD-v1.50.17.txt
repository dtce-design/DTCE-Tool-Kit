PATCH: jamb columns set by the program.

- Door & Window Schedule ("pwede ba alisin ito? sa system na lang? yung jamb depth based na lang sa chb or masonry"): the
  Jamb depth, Jamb thick and Leaf thickness columns are hidden. The program sets them:
  - jamb depth = thickness of the CHB / masonry wall the opening is assigned to (Wall ID or opening assignment): CHB 100 or
    150 mm, AAC 100 or 150 mm, and so on; an opening with no wall uses the "Unassigned CHB thickness" setting;
  - jamb thickness = the jamb thickness setting (50 mm default); wooden door leaf = 45 mm.
  Changing the CHB / masonry thickness updates the jamb depth and jamb paint on the next calculation. An opening assigned to
  walls of different thickness asks for separate opening IDs, as before.
- Saved projects open unchanged (the values stay in the same columns).

Includes v1.50.16 and earlier local builds (see their notes).
