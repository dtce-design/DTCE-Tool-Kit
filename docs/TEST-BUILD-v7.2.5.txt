PATCH - Strap Foundation: ground water table and permissible loss of contact (owner: "naconsider ba GWT sa strap design?"; "include
permissible loss of contact"):
- New inputs: Ground water table below grade (m, blank = none), Required FS against flotation (1.1), Minimum contact under wind /
  seismic cases (% of base, 50). Older sheet files open with no water table.
- Water table above the footing base: the water pressure on the base, U = γw (Df - GWT) B L, is deducted from the footing + soil
  weight, so bearing, contact, sliding and overturning use the reduced weight. New check per footing: Flotation, FS = (footing + soil
  + smallest service column load) / U >= the required FS (strap weight not counted).
- Soil takes no tension: when the linear pressure gives q min < 0, the pressure q = max(0, a + b x + c z) is found that still carries
  the resultant and both moments (biaxial, iterated on a 60 x 60 grid); q max is its peak (checked against qa and used for the footing
  design) and the contact is the part of the base in compression. One-way case equals the closed form 2R / (3B(L/2 - e)).
- Contact checks: gravity service cases need the whole base in contact; service cases with wind or seismic (W, WX, WL, WIND, E, EQ,
  EQX, SEISMIC ... in the load case name) may lose contact down to the minimum contact. Check cards show the linear q min, the
  redistributed peak and the contact %.
- Report: the water table and contact rule in the materials table (with the cases taken as wind / seismic); statics table has q min
  linear and Contact columns, partial-contact rows tinted.
Tests: StrapFootingTests 80 (no-tension peak and contact against the closed form, wind / seismic detection, water uplift, net weight,
flotation check).
